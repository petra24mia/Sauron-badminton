About the club

Run by students, on university courts

Sauron is the student badminton association at Tilburg University, and one of the racket sports clubs based at the Sports Center. Training is organised across three levels, so you're grouped with players working on similar things — whether that's learning to count a rally and hold a racket properly, or drilling attacking and defensive doubles tactics for league matches.

Alongside organised training, there's plenty of room to just play — the Sports Center keeps daytime slots open for members who want to book a court independently.

The club competes in the regional league, with the top squad currently playing third division.

Training groups

Three levels, one club

Registration is required to join level 1 and 2 training groups.

Level 3 · Beginners

Learning the fundamentals

Footwork, grip, scoring, and stroke technique for each shot, plus match play as you go. The right starting point if you're new to the sport or haven't played competitively before.

Level 2 · Intermediate

Building match tactics

For players with some league experience who already have the basic strokes down. Focus shifts to doubles tactics, attacking and defensive positioning, and singles strategy.

Level 1 · Competitive

Training the top teams

Technical and tactical training for the club's highest teams, including circuit and speed work and specialty strokes like the cut smash, drop, flick serve, and drive.

How to join

Three steps to your first session

Membership runs per semester. New to the sport? Come try a session first — just email us so we know to expect you.

  1. Fill in the registration form

    Name, student number, and which level fits you best. Takes about two minutes.

  2. Pay the membership fee

    Covers your spot in training and league costs where relevant. Payment instructions are on the confirmation page.

  3. Check the sports schedule and show up

    Training times and courts are listed on the Sports Center's schedule — you'll also get added to the club group chat for any changes.
